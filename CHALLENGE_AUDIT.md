## Organizer Q&A decisions applied

The supplied Q&A transcript is traceable by line references in `src/data/organizer-guidance.json`; the desk displays and exports that same coverage record.

- RR limits remain the main Space source; worst-case full gain towards Finland is accepted. Missing GIMS footprints alone are **not** a blocker for this labelled assumption.
- Excel 9/10 kHz mismatches use the smaller value; station 440557 uses the same minimum-bandwidth policy while retaining raw values. No hidden source edits.
- P.1546-6 is accepted as a final basis. P.452/HCM and the optional 10 m→3 m correction are **not** required to finish this challenge.
- General A1.2.3 is the challenge choice above 470 MHz; it is not a universal CEPT rule and does not extend P.1546 beyond its own domain.
- Finnish **land borders and outer territorial-sea limit**, plus exposed Excel stations, are required; coastlines/EEZ or one representative site do not substitute.
- Reported coordination with Finland and favourable BR findings never silently close a calculated concern; registry verification is independent of technical screening.
- Ordinary documented geographic datasets and Space evaluation grids are accepted. Team review buffers are allowed, documented and modifiable; no synthetic band gets preferred priority.
- CSV/XLS or document outputs are preferred; machine-readable JSON is welcome. No organizer-supplied case labels or special P.1546 reference set are available; this does not validate our cases automatically.
